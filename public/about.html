About Earny Day

Complete tasks. Play games. Earn real cash.

Every day, millions of people spend hours on their phones — scrolling, tapping, waiting. Earny Day was built on a simple idea: that time can be worth something more. Not a fortune, not a shortcut to riches, but a small, honest reward for the small, honest moments you already spend online.

What Earny Day Is

Earny Day is a mobile-first platform where completing everyday digital tasks turns into real earnings. Sign up for a service you were already curious about, try a free trial, answer a short survey about your opinions, or play a quick mini-game during a break. Each of these becomes a small deposit into your wallet — coins that convert directly into rupees, withdrawable straight to your UPI account.

We didn't want to build another app that promises the world and delivers nothing. We wanted something transparent: you do a task, you see exactly what it pays, you get paid.

How It Works

The Tasks page brings together offers from multiple advertising networks — sign-ups, app installs, free trials, and more — each showing its exact coin reward up front. The Surveys section connects you with question-and-answer surveys that pay per completed response.

Beyond tasks, Earny Day features a full slate of tournament mini-games, built from scratch with real design and polish. Puzzle games, memory games, reaction tests, word games, and more. Join a room, compete against other players in real time, and the highest score wins coins — no entry fee required.

Daily Habits, Daily Rewards

Earny Day includes a daily check-in streak, a spin wheel, and a scratch card — small daily rituals that add a few coins to your balance just for showing up.

Withdrawals You Can Trust

Every withdrawal goes straight to a UPI ID of your choice — the same UPI you already use with Google Pay, PhonePe, or any other app. There's no unfamiliar payment gateway to trust, and every user can track their withdrawal history right inside the app.

Built for Trust

We know the earning-app space has a reputation problem. Too many platforms overpromise, underpay, or disappear once they've collected enough downloads. Earny Day was built to be the opposite of that — every reward shown in the app is calculated from a real partner payout, and every feature is tested carefully before going live.
