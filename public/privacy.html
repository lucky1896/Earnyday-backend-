Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Earny Day ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely, hashed).
  • Wallet information: UPI ID, when you submit one for a withdrawal or Premium payment.
  • Usage data: tasks completed, games played, and coins earned, so your balance can be tracked accurately.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, and general usage patterns, collected automatically for security and analytics.

How We Use Information

We use your information to operate your account, calculate and credit your earnings, process withdrawal and Premium payment requests, prevent fraud, and improve the platform based on how it's actually used.

Third-Party Advertising and Partners

Earny Day displays advertising through Google AdSense and works with third-party task, survey, and offer networks (including MyLead, CPX Research, and AdGate Media) to provide earning opportunities. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads and to verify when you've completed a task, in order to credit your reward correctly.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what's necessary with payment processors and task/offer networks to operate the service, and we may disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal and financial record-keeping obligations.

Your Choices

You can update your account details within the app, and you can request account deletion by contacting us. Withdrawal and earnings records may be retained separately for legal and accounting purposes even after account deletion.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support options available inside the Earny Day app.
